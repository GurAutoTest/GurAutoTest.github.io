When you get your money back, and when we reprint instead.

In short

Stickers are printed with your own code, so they cannot be resold. That is why we do not take returns for a change of mind — but if something is our fault, we fix it at no cost to you.

Cancelling an order

  • Before printing — cancel from WhatsApp or email and you get a full refund, including delivery.
  • After printing — the stickers carry your code, so the order cannot be cancelled.

If something is wrong

If your stickers arrive damaged, misprinted, or not what you ordered, send us a photo within 7 days of delivery. We reprint and send them again free — the same code, so nothing changes on your side. If we cannot reprint, we refund the order in full.

Lost or worn-out stickers

Not a fault, so not a refund — but not a problem either. We reprint the same code and send a new pair; you pay only delivery. Every sticker you still have keeps working.

If a parcel does not arrive

If the courier loses your parcel, we reprint and send it again free, or refund you in full — your choice. If a parcel comes back because the address was wrong or nobody accepted it, we can send it again for the delivery charge.

The free pack

The pack itself costs nothing, so the only money is the delivery charge. It is refunded only if the order is cancelled before printing, or if the parcel is lost by the courier.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the way you paid — UPI, card or bank — within 5–7 working days of us approving them.
