01 · When someone scans you

Scan. Tap. Saved.

No app, no typing your number twice, no “wait, spell that again”.

One page, everything on it

Number, WhatsApp, email, socials, your shop’s map — one link instead of five things to spell out.

Call or WhatsApp in one tap

Nobody types your number wrong ever again. One tap and they’re calling or chatting.

Saved to contacts in one tap

One tap on Save contact and you’re in their phone — not lost in a gallery of business-card photos.

Twelve looks

Clean white, bold colour, glass, your own photo. Switch whenever you feel like it.

Any camera, no app

Every phone camera reads it. Nothing to download for the person scanning you.

Show only what you want

Hide your number on a busy day and keep WhatsApp. One tap per row, no deleting.

02 · Keep in touch

Met someone? Keep the thread.

Say hi without swapping numbers, and find each other again later.

New

Quick Chat

People who scan you can message you inside Codemarca — not on your phone or WhatsApp. You choose who gets a reply.

New

Follow

Liked someone’s sticker? Follow them and their page is one tap away next time. No feed, no noise — just a list.

New

Scan to say hi first

Spot a sticker you like? Scan it from your Codemarca and start the chat yourself.

Real people only

Everyone signs in with Google before they can message, and a full chat needs a page of their own.

03 · When something goes missing

Lost it? Your sticker still knows who to call.

The page changes the moment you need it to — even from a friend’s phone.

New

Lost mode

Phone, bag or keys gone? Switch it on and whoever finds it sees who to call, right at the top.

New

Travel mode

Going on a trip? Add up to two people a finder can call and a short note. It switches itself off when you’re back.

Numbers stay hidden till tapped

Contacts on the banner show as 98xxx xx310 until someone taps to call — and calling needs no sign-up.

Switch it on from any device

Your phone is the thing that’s missing, so sign in from a friend’s phone or a laptop and turn it on there.

04 · You’re in control

Your page, your rules.

Every switch is yours, and nothing is shared that you didn’t put there.

Switch anything off

Don’t want chats or followers? Turn them off and your page shows only your contacts.

Chats that clean themselves

Seen messages disappear 24 hours after they’re seen. Want them gone now? Clear the chat.

500 characters a day

Every chat shares one small daily limit, so people get to the point and spam has nowhere to go.

Block and report

Block someone quietly — they’re not told. Report anything off and our team takes a look.

Pause your page

Switch the page off any time. Your stickers stay put and come back to life when you switch it on.

Views only you can see

See how many people opened your page, and how many came from the sticker. Nobody else sees it.

05 · The sticker

Stuck once. Works for good.

A small square of vinyl that keeps up with every change you make.

One code for life

New number, new Instagram, new name — edit the page and every sticker you’ve stuck shows the new one.

Free pack, two sizes

A 4″ for the car or shop and a 1.5″ for your laptop or phone. You only pay delivery. More sizes when you want them.

Rain-proof, sun-proof

UV-laminated vinyl that survives rain, sun and the car wash.

Lost a sticker? Same code again

We reprint the same code and you pay only delivery. Everything else you’ve stuck keeps working.

Gift a code

Order stickers for someone else. The code becomes theirs the moment they scan it and sign in.

50 friends, free tee

Friends who sign up with your link and finish their page count. Hit 50 and a tee with your own QR is on us.

One page for everything you are — printed as a QR you can stick anywhere.
